# Home — AI Tools

The Home tab is where you run AI on your resume.

## What you can do

- Upload your resume.
- Run one of three tools:
  - **Analyze** — review your resume and get feedback.
  - **Cover Letter** — write a cover letter for a job.
  - **ATS Check** — score your resume against a job.

## Steps

1. Open the **Home** tab.
2. Upload your resume (tap the uploader).
3. Paste the job description if the tool needs one.
4. Tap the tool you want.
5. Wait for the AI to finish.
6. See the result, or open the **Analyze** tab for the full view.

## Tips

- The token badge at the top shows how many AI runs you have left.
- If something fails, the app shows an error you can dismiss and retry.
