# Profile & Tokens

Your account and usage.

## What you see

- Your name and email.
- Your plan: **Free**, **Pro**, or **Admin**.
- Your token balance (how many AI runs you have left).

## Steps

1. Open the **Profile** tab.
2. Check your plan and token count.
3. Tap **Log Out** when you want to sign out.

## About tokens

- Each AI action (analyze, cover letter, ATS) uses tokens.
- When you run low, upgrade your plan to get more.
