# Analyze

Shows the results from your AI runs in one place.

## Three sub-tabs

- **Analysis** — feedback on your resume.
- **Cover Letter** — the generated cover letter.
- **ATS Score** — your score, strengths, gaps, and suggestions.

## Steps

1. Run a tool on the **Home** tab first.
2. Open the **Analyze** tab.
3. Tap a sub-tab to switch between views.

## Tips

- The ATS view lists strengths and gaps so you know what to fix.
- Copy the cover letter text to use in your application.
