# Tracker

Keep a list of the jobs you applied to.

## Statuses

Each job has a status: **Applied**, **Interview**, **Offer**, or **Rejected**.

## Steps

1. Open the **Tracker** tab.
2. Tap add to create a new job entry.
3. Fill in the details (company, role, status).
4. Save.
5. Use the filter at the top to show only one status.

## Tips

- Update the status as you move through interviews.
- Your list saves to your account, so it stays in sync.
