# Sign In & Register

How to get into the app.

## Sign in

1. Open the app.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Tap **Log In**.

## Register

1. On the login screen, tap to create an account.
2. Enter your email and a password.
3. Submit to create the account.

## Tips

- Use the same account as the web app to share your data.
- If a field is empty, the app reminds you before continuing.
