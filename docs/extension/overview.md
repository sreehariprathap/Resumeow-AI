# Chrome Extension — Overview

The extension lives in your browser and auto-fills job applications for you.

Full name: **Resumeow — Smart Job Autofill**.

## What it does

- Detects when you are on a job page.
- Scans the job (title, company, description).
- Matches it to one of your resumes.
- Auto-fills the application form with AI.

## How to start

1. Install the extension and pin it to your toolbar.
2. Click the icon and sign in.
3. Visit a job page on a supported site.
4. The extension detects the job and shows the details.
5. Pick a resume and let it fill the form.

## Good to know

- It uses the same account as the web and mobile apps.
- It only acts on job pages. On other pages it stays quiet.
