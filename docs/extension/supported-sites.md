# Supported Sites

Where the extension can scan jobs and auto-fill forms.

## Sites

- Workday (deepest support)
- Greenhouse
- Lever
- LinkedIn
- Indeed

## Notes

- Workday has the strongest support, including multi-step application forms.
- On other sites the extension stays quiet and does nothing.
- Be on the actual job posting or application form, not a search results page.

If your site is not listed, the extension will show a "not on a job page" message.
