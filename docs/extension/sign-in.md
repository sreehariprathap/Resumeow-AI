# Sign In

You sign in once so the extension can use your resumes.

## Steps

1. Click the Resumeow icon in your toolbar.
2. The popup shows a sign-in screen.
3. Sign in with your account.
4. You stay signed in for next time.

## Tips

- Use the same account as the web app so your resumes are available.
- If the popup asks you to sign in again, just repeat these steps.
