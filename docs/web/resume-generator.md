# Resume Generator

Builds a clean, professional resume from your details.

## Steps

1. Open **Resume Generator**.
2. Check your details are filled in (name, experience, skills).
3. Click **Build My Resume**.
4. The AI writes your resume using the LaTeX "Jake's Resume" template.
5. Review it on the right side.
6. Copy or export the result.

## Tips

- The output is LaTeX, which gives a tidy, ATS-friendly layout.
- Want it aimed at a specific job? Use [JD Matcher](./jd-matcher.md) or [Lazy Mode](./lazy-mode.md) instead.
