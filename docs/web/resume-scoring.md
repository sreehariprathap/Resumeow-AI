# Resume Scoring

Grades your resume and tells you what to fix.

## What you get

- A score out of 100 and a letter grade.
- A breakdown by area (keywords, skills, format, and more).
- Clear suggestions to improve.

## Steps

1. Open **Resume Scoring**.
2. Load or paste your resume.
3. Wait while the AI scores it.
4. Read the score breakdown.
5. Click **Fix Resume** to apply suggested improvements.

## Tips

- Aim for 80+ before applying.
- Fix the lowest-scoring area first for the biggest gain.
