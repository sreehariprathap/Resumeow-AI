# Web App — Overview

The web app is the full version of Resumeow. Use it on a laptop or desktop.

## What it does

- Builds a resume tailored to a job.
- Scores your resume and tells you how to improve it.
- Matches your resume against a job description (JD).
- Writes cover letters.
- Runs a one-click "Lazy Mode" that does everything for you.
- Saves your templates and history to your account.

## How to start

1. Open the web app in your browser.
2. Sign in with Google (or email).
3. First time only: finish the short onboarding. You can upload a resume and the AI fills your details for you.
4. Pick a tool from the menu and go.
