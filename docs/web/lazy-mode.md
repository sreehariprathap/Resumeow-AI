# Lazy Mode

The fastest way to use Resumeow. Paste a job, get everything back.

## What you get

- A match score for your resume vs. the job.
- Key insights and missing keywords.
- A tailored resume, ready to view.

## Steps

1. Open **Lazy Mode**.
2. Paste the job description.
3. Press start.
4. Watch the AI pipeline run (it shows each step as it works).
5. When done, review the score and insights.
6. Click **View Resume** to see the tailored resume.

## Tips

- The score is out of 100. Green means a strong match.
- If the score is low, check the missing keywords and add the real ones to your profile.
