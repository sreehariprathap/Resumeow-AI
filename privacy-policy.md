# Privacy Policy - Prompter Resume & Cover Letter Assistant

**Last Updated: June 3, 2025**

## 1. Introduction

Resumeow ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our Resume & Cover Letter Assistant web application.

By using Prompter, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information
- **Google Account Information**: When you sign in with Google, we collect:
  - Name
  - Email address
  - Profile picture
  - Google user ID (for authentication purposes)

### 2.2 Resume and Professional Content
- **Resume Data**: Text content, formatting, and structure of your resumes
- **Cover Letter Content**: Text and formatting of cover letters you create
- **Job Descriptions**: Job posting content you input for analysis
- **Templates**: Custom templates you create or modify
- **Prompt Configurations**: Your saved prompt templates and preferences

### 2.3 Usage Data
- **Application Interactions**: Features used, buttons clicked, and navigation patterns
- **ATS Analysis Results**: Scores, feedback, and optimization suggestions
- **AI API Usage**: Frequency and type of AI-powered features used
- **Session Information**: Login times, session duration, and activity timestamps

### 2.4 Technical Data
- **Browser Information**: Browser type, version, and user agent
- **Device Data**: Operating system, screen resolution, and device type
- **IP Address**: For security and analytics purposes
- **Local Storage**: Preferences and temporary data stored in your browser

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 Core Functionality
- **Authentication**: Verify your identity and maintain secure sessions
- **Content Storage**: Save your resumes, cover letters, and templates
- **ATS Analysis**: Analyze resume compatibility with job requirements
- **AI Processing**: Generate optimized prompts and content suggestions
- **Template Management**: Store and sync your custom templates across devices

### 3.2 Service Improvement
- **Performance Optimization**: Analyze usage patterns to improve app performance
- **Feature Development**: Understand user needs to develop new features
- **Error Tracking**: Identify and fix technical issues
- **Analytics**: Aggregate usage statistics for service improvement

### 3.3 Communication
- **Service Updates**: Notify you of important changes or new features
- **Support**: Respond to your questions and provide assistance
- **Security Alerts**: Inform you of security-related matters

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Storage Locations
- **Firebase Firestore**: User profiles, templates, and application data
- **Firebase Authentication**: Secure user authentication tokens
- **Local Browser Storage**: Temporary data and user preferences
- **Third-Party AI Services**: Temporary processing of content for analysis

### 4.2 Security Measures
- **Encryption**: All data transmitted is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
- **Firebase Security Rules**: Database access restricted to authenticated users
- **Authentication Tokens**: Secure, time-limited session management
- **Data Isolation**: User data is isolated and not shared between accounts
- **Regular Security Audits**: Ongoing security assessment and improvements

### 4.3 Data Retention
- **Active Accounts**: Data retained while your account is active
- **Account Deletion**: Data permanently deleted within 30 days of account deletion
- **Inactive Accounts**: Data may be deleted after 2 years of inactivity
- **Legal Requirements**: Some data may be retained longer for legal compliance

## 14. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Is my resume data private?
**A:** Yes, your resume data is private and only accessible to you. We use it only to provide our services and never share it with third parties.

### Q: Can I use Prompter without creating an account?
**A:** Limited functionality is available without an account, but full features require authentication for data persistence and synchronization.

### Q: How do I delete my account and data?
**A:** You can delete your account through the Settings menu. All associated data will be permanently removed within 30 days.

### Q: Do you use my data to train AI models?
**A:** No, we do not use your personal data or content to train AI models. Your data is processed only to provide our services.

### Q: Is my data backed up?
**A:** Yes, your data is automatically backed up through Firebase's infrastructure, ensuring reliability and disaster recovery.

### Q: Can I export my data?
**A:** Yes, you can export your templates, resumes, and other content through the application's export features.
